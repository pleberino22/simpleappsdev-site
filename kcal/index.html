Kcal

One number. Three macros. No noise.

Log what you eat. See your calories and macros. Nothing else.

$0.99 one-time, after a 7-day free trial. No subscription.

01

One ring

Your whole day on one screen: remaining calories in the center, protein, fat and carbs beneath, four fixed meals — Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack. Swipe back through any past day.

02

Five ways to log

Scan a barcode, search a food database, snap a photo or describe your meal in words for an AI estimate (10 a day), or type it in yourself. Keep your own foods and recipes in a small library; recent foods are one tap away.

03

Streaks, not guilt

A flame counts your consecutive logged days. The calendar marks days within budget. Forget a meal and Kcal nudges you once — one switch, no schedule to configure.

What Kcal refuses to do

  • No fasting timer.
  • No recipe feed.
  • No step counting.
  • No water log.
  • No social anything.

Crossed off on purpose.

Kcal provides general estimates, not medical advice. Consult a professional for personal guidance.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
