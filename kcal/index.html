Kcal

Your day. Made simple.

Log your food. See what’s left. Get on with your day.

$0.99 optional one-time unlock after 7 days free. No subscription or automatic charge. Local prices are shown in the app.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, shortcuts, favorite portions with Undo, and a clearer setup. The update has been submitted to Apple; these additions are not in the current App Store release yet.

01

Your day, clearly

Remaining calories, protein, fat and carbs, plus four meal cards on one fixed screen. Browse earlier days and choose goals that suit you.

02

Less effort to log

Scan, search, photograph or type your food. Use Quick calories when you know the value, keep favorite foods and remembered portions, or review and copy a meal from another day.

03

A diary you keep

Your foods, recipes and history stay on your iPhone. Export a backup and preview it before restoring; existing entries are kept. No account, ads or subscription.

What Kcal refuses to do

  • No fasting timer.
  • No recipe feed.
  • No step counting.
  • No water log.
  • No social anything.

Crossed off on purpose.

The free trial starts when you first open Kcal. After 7 days, choose whether to unlock logging with one purchase. Your existing diary stays readable either way.

AI photo estimates, meal descriptions and nutrition-label reads share 10 successful requests a day, during the trial and after purchase. AI sharing needs your permission, and every estimate is editable before saving.

Kcal provides general estimates, not medical advice. Consult a professional for personal guidance.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
