What happens after the 7-day trial?

The trial starts when you first open Kcal. After 7 days, you can choose a one-time unlock to keep logging. It costs $0.99 in the US; your local price is shown in the app before purchase. There is no subscription or automatic charge and nothing to cancel. If you do not buy, your existing diary stays readable.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Kcal’s Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple account you bought the unlock with. Restoring the purchase restores access; to move diary entries to a new phone, use a Kcal backup or your device backup.

How can I log faster?

Use Quick calories when you know the calorie value and do not need to enter macros. Save foods as favorites and reuse remembered portions, or choose Copy meal to review an earlier meal before adding a copy to another day. Your own foods and recipes are in Food Library.

How do I back up or move my diary?

Open Settings → Backup & restore, choose Export backup and save the file somewhere private. On the other phone, choose Restore from backup and review the preview before merging. Existing data is kept and identical records are skipped. Goals and appearance are only restored if you choose to apply those settings. A diary backup does not transfer your purchase; use Restore purchase separately.

Where are the widgets and one-tap favorite buttons?

These are part of Kcal 1.3, which has been submitted to Apple. The website images preview that update; it is not in the current App Store release yet. Once 1.3 is released and installed, open Kcal once, then add a widget from your iPhone’s Home Screen or Lock Screen. The update also adds app-icon actions and Shortcuts.

The scanner cannot find my product

Allow camera access in iPhone Settings → Kcal. Some products are missing from the food databases; search by name, read the nutrition label or enter the food manually instead.

How do nutrition-label photos work?

Photograph the printed nutrition table and review the calories, protein, fat and carbs before saving. You enter the food’s name yourself. Per-serving labels need a printed gram weight for conversion. Labels per 100 ml, or servings stated only in cups or pieces, cannot be converted to grams safely; enter those manually using a weighed serving.

Why do I only get ten AI requests a day?

Meal-photo estimates, typed meal descriptions and nutrition-label reads share 10 successful requests per day. Failed requests do not use one. The limit resets at midnight and is the same during the trial and after purchase. If the service asks you to wait, try again later; buying the unlock does not remove this allowance.

Can I stop sharing photos or descriptions with AI?

Yes. Withdraw AI-sharing permission in Settings. AI entry will ask for permission before sending selected content again. Your existing diary remains available, and manual entry does not need AI sharing.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual diary entries, foods or recipes in Kcal, or delete the app to remove its local data. Also delete any exported backups you no longer need; removing the app does not remove files saved elsewhere. Kcal has no account to close.
