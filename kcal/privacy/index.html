Privacy

Your food diary lives on your iPhone. Here is what is stored, what is shared, and what you control.

Your diary stays on your iPhone

Foods, meals, goals, recipes, favorites and diary history are stored locally. Kcal does not upload your full diary to a developer-run server. There is no Kcal account or cloud diary service.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Barcode scanning sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts and, if needed, FatSecret. The camera image is not sent for a barcode lookup.
  • Food search sends your search terms to USDA FoodData Central and FatSecret to find foods and nutrition values.
  • Nutrition-label reads and meal-photo estimates send the selected photo to Groq. Meal descriptions send the text you enter to Groq. Kcal asks for permission before this AI sharing; you can withdraw it in Settings. It does not include your full diary, goals or history. The returned values remain editable before saving.

Kcal does not add an advertising ID, your Apple ID or account details to these requests. Online services still receive connection information needed to handle a request, such as your IP address; their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used for barcodes, nutrition labels and meal photos. For label reads and meal estimates, you may choose an existing photo instead. Kcal only receives the photo you select, not your whole photo library. It does not build a photo album. If you decline camera access, manual logging and other entry options remain available.

Backups you choose to export

Backup files contain diary and food data, recipes, favorites, goals and preferences. They do not include payment credentials, purchase entitlement, the trial start, AI-sharing permission or notification permission. You choose where to save or share them. A file saved to cloud storage or sent to someone leaves the app under your control, so keep backups somewhere private.

Restoring first shows a preview and merges the backup with your current diary. Existing entries are not deleted; identical records are skipped. Deleting Kcal does not delete backup files you exported elsewhere.

Widgets in the upcoming 1.3 update

Widgets use a local summary of daily calorie and macro totals. Full diary entries and food names are not shared with the widget. This summary is kept on your device; the widget does not send it to an online service. Kcal 1.3 has been submitted to Apple.

Notifications

Meal reminders are optional. Your iPhone schedules them locally, and they do not report your meals to a server. Turning reminders off cancels them.

Trial and purchases

The 7-day trial starts the first time you open Kcal and is tracked locally. The optional one-time unlock is purchased through Apple. There is no subscription or automatic charge at the end of the trial. Kcal checks purchase entitlement; the developer does not receive your card details or Apple ID.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

Kcal contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps. Optional online lookups and AI requests work as described above.

Deleting your data

Remove individual entries, foods and recipes inside Kcal, or delete the app to remove its local data. There is no Kcal account to close. Separately delete any exported backups or copies you no longer want. Device backups managed by Apple are controlled through your iPhone or Apple account settings.
