01

The window, not the date

Everything you track carries its own notice period — the month a cancellation needs, the weeks a passport takes. Lapse draws both ends on the calendar and threads them together, so you see the day to start as clearly as the day it's due.

02

A month you can read

A hollow ring is the day to act, a filled dot is the day itself. Under the calendar sits one number: what actually leaves your account this month. Tap it for the monthly average instead.

03

Everything that quietly runs out

Subscriptions, passports, insurance, warranties, contracts — sorted into categories you can rename, on whatever cycle they really run, in whatever currency you pay them.

What Lapse refuses to do

  • No charts.
  • No budgets.
  • No payment history.
  • No account.
  • No streaks.

Crossed off on purpose.
