One reminder. Not one per pill.

Group what you take, take it in one tap, and know when to reorder.

Coming soon

$0.99 one-time, after a 7-day free trial. No subscription.

01

Stacks, not pills

Group what you take into named stacks — Morning, Post-workout, Night — and give each one its times and days. One reminder for the whole group, however many things are in it.

02

Take it, or don't

Take the whole stack at once, or check off item by item and leave the rest. Skip what you didn't take. Tapped the wrong thing? Open it again and undo it — your stock comes back with it.

03

Never run out

Every dose you take comes off your count, so each item shows about how many days are left and tells you while there's still time to reorder. Anything you'd rather not count, leave untracked.

What Dose refuses to do

  • No adherence score.
  • No streaks.
  • No history to scroll.
  • No account.
  • No ads, no tracking.

Crossed off on purpose.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
